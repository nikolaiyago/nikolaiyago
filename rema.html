Cashier Interface Redesign

Interaction design - Food / January 2026

Tags: Information design Analysing Psychology Strategic design

Duration: 1 week

Every day, thousands of cashiers in Norway use a system that is outdated and poorly designed. How is it possible that in 2026, we still don't make these tools better for the people who use them most.

The task was to select an existing digital interface, identify where the user experience breaks down, recreate the original, and redesign it — justifying every decision through UX principles and interaction design theory. I chose the REMA 1000 cashier interface because I use it every day. New employees consistently struggle with it — common tasks require unnecessary memorisation, slow down the checkout process, and increase the chance of errors. That was the problem worth solving.
