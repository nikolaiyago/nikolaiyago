Our Fjord, Our Future

Systems-oriented design - Sustainability & environment / August–October 2025

Tags: Systems thinking Collaboration Sustainability Civic engagement Regenerative Design

Duration: 7 weeks

Connect solutions to save the Oslo Fjord.

This project was initiated by our teacher, Abel, who connected us with organisations working on the Oslofjord watershed, including NIVA and Pådriv Oslo.

We researched on site and in workshops, mapped stakeholders, initiatives and pain points, and used scenarios and visual methods to make the system easier to grasp.

We came to see the river as an interconnected system — and how design can help communicate its challenges to a wider audience.
