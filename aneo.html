“ANEO”

Interaction design - Sustainability & environment / June 2026

Tags: Systems mapping Strategic design Business design Analysing Psychology

Duration: —

Spotify built a way to listen together. Almost no one uses it, and the few who find it are faced with some challenges.

The task was to take one feature of a music streaming app, find a real gap, and design a focused improvement we could back up with evidence. We picked Jam, Spotify's collaborative listening feature, and went after the part that breaks first: getting people into a session. We audited how the major apps handle shared listening, interviewed people about how they actually listen together, prototyped a simpler way to start and join a Jam, and tested it with real users. Along the way we learned that the hard problem wasn't the listening — it was everything that happens before the music starts.

00 Challenge & my role

Challenge

The brief was to pick a single feature area within music streaming, map what already exists, identify an underserved need, and design a focused solution, backed by research and tested with real users.

My role

Part of a team of three. We shared the analysis phase, each mapping different music streaming platforms before aligning on our findings. Together we chose to focus on music listening in social settings, inspired by our own experiences with Spotify Jam.

My specific contribution was conducting interviews, drawing and documenting our process, and making sure our conversations and decisions were captured on paper so nothing was lost between sessions.
